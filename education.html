The programme

Investing in our people

The Tutumatai Station Education Grant Programme was established in 2025 to provide financial support to descendants of Raana Te Iti Walker and Mere Roa Milner who are enrolled in tertiary study.

Eligibility & criteria

Who can apply

  • All descendants of Raana Te Iti Walker and Mere Roa Milner are eligible to apply
  • Applicants must be enrolled in a course of tertiary study at a University, Polytechnic, Wānanga, or approved private training establishment
  • The course must lead to a qualification or further opportunity — including trades and apprenticeships
  • Evidence of enrolment or acceptance into the course must be provided
  • Repeat applicants must provide evidence of achievement from the previous year
  • Applicants developing farming and farm management skills are particularly encouraged
  • Grants are awarded at the discretion of the Committee of Management and are subject to available funds

Apply for a grant

Applications open annually. Descendants studying in tertiary education are encouraged to apply before 31 March each year.
